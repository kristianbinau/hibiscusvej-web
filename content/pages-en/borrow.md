## Hammer Drill & Screwdriver

The property has a communal hammer drill and screwdriver which all residents can borrow by writing to the caretaker Peter at plj@abodense.dk.

Please note that you should expect a reply on weekdays from 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM.

## Communal room

All residents have access to and can book the communal room at Hibiscusvej 22.
