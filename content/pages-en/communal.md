## Cleaning

Cleaning equipment is available by the kitchen, including reusable cloths, tea towels and sponges. After use, these should be placed in the pink basket so they can be washed.

## Rules for renting the communal room

- Your booking of the room is valid from 10:00 AM on the selected day until 10:00 AM the following day.
- Furniture, tableware and any other items found in the room must be returned in the same condition as they were at the start of your booking.
- The room must be cleared and cleaned before your booking ends. This includes:
  - Tidying up.
  - Sweeping and washing the floor.
  - Emptying all rubbish bins.
  - Cleaning the dishes and emptying the dishwasher.
- If anything is missing from the room or it has not been cleaned since the previous booking, please contact the local residents' board.
- If you wish to use the room in the evening and/or play music, you MUST book the room.
- Overnight stays or sleeping in the communal room are not permitted.
- The community rules for Gartnerbyen also apply to this room, including the rules regarding music and loud noise:  
  Avoid playing music with doors and windows open, or making noise in a way that disturbs your neighbours. Special consideration must be given between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM. On Fridays and Saturdays, parties and gatherings may continue until midnight. If an event continues beyond midnight, conversations and music must be kept at a low volume so as not to disturb nearby residents.
- Violations of the above rules will result in immediate removal from the room and a minimum ban of 6 months.
