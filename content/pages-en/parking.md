## Parking

Parking is managed by [APCOA](https://www.apcoa.dk/).

We have 2 parking areas.

- Green spaces, which are right next to the building.
- Extra spaces, which are located a bit further away.

An individual parking permit is required for each of the 2 zones in order to park.  
Parking is at your own risk; if you do not have a valid parking permit, you risk receiving a parking charge.  
AB Odense or the residents' board does not cover the cost of any parking charge.

#### Green spaces

The green spaces are located right next to the building, and there are 11 spaces available.  
Please note that there is a single space in the blue zone which we are not allowed to use.  
_Space number_: 3930

#### Extra spaces

The extra spaces are located at the end of Lavsenvænget, where there is a large car park in which we have 4 spaces.  
The spaces are right at the end of the car park, and there is a sign showing that these are our spaces. <a href="/images/parking-sign.webp" target="_blank">Click to see the parking sign</a>  
_Space number_: 4744

## Parking permit

Parking permits can be obtained by writing to [ab@abodense.dk](mailto:ab@abodense.dk).

It is free of charge, and you can update your number plate yourself if you change car.  
It is only permitted to have one parking permit, per zone, per household.

To obtain a parking permit, you must provide:

- Name
- Address
- Number plate
- Area
  - 3930 (Green spaces)
  - 4744 (Extra spaces)

Please note that the request will be sent to AB Odense for approval.  
You will receive an **individual confirmation per zone** by email once your request has been approved.

## Guest parking

Guests cannot park in our parking spaces.  
There are guest parking spaces around Gartnerbyen, which can be used for a fee.
