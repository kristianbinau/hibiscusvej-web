# Privacy policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use and protect your information.

## Collection of information

We only collect personal information when you voluntarily provide it to us, for example when you:

- Create a user account.
- Update your contact details.

The information we typically collect may include:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Address

## Use of information

We use your information to:

- Identify you as a user.
- Contact you regarding the use of communal facilities.
- Verify that you are entitled to use the communal facilities.

We do not share your personal information with third parties, unless it is required by law, or you have given us your explicit consent.

## Storage of information

We store your information for as long as you are a user of our website. If you wish to delete your account, please contact us, and we will delete your information.

## Cookies

We do not use any cookies on our website.

## Third parties

We use third-party services to help us manage our website. These services are committed to protecting your information and are not allowed to share it with others. The services include:

- [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/gdpr/)
  - Server hosting and security.
- [NuxtHub](https://admin.hub.nuxt.com/privacy)
  - Server administration and maintenance.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Access the information we hold about you.
- Have incorrect information corrected.
- Have your information deleted (under certain conditions).
- Withdraw your consent (if relevant).

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you are welcome to contact us at:

gdpr@binau.me

## Changes to the privacy policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
